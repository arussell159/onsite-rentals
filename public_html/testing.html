Frequently asked questions

What happens if I don't have an ECTN?

Without a valid ECTN, your cargo can be delayed at the destination port and may be subject to fines or penalties.

When should I apply for an ECTN?

Apply at least 5 business days before the validation deadline so there is enough time to review, approve, and validate the certificate.

How much does an ECTN cost?

The cost depends on the destination country and shipment details. AfricaCTN can provide a quote once the shipment information is available.

Is ECTN the same as CTN or BESC?

Yes. ECTN, CTN, BESC, BSC, FERI, and similar names refer to cargo tracking documents used by different countries and authorities.

Which countries require an ECTN?

ECTN or related CTN documents are required by many African destinations, including Angola, Benin, Cameroon, DR Congo, Gabon, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Madagascar, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Sudan, Togo, and others.

Who issues or validates the ECTN?

In most countries, local port authorities validate the ECTN. Where authorized agents are appointed, AfricaCTN can serve as the official validating authority for the CTN.

How long does validation of an ECTN take?

After the draft is approved and the invoice is paid, validation is typically submitted to the relevant authority and often takes 24 to 48 hours, depending on the country and situation.

How to get your ECTN?

Send your documents by email or through the CTN Dashboard, receive a draft CTN and invoice after review, then receive the validated certificate once the draft is submitted and released.

Does each Bill of Lading need its own ECTN number?

Yes. Each bill of lading needs its own unique ECTN number; one CTN cannot be filed for multiple bills of lading.

What documents are required to get an ECTN?

Most applications require the bill of lading, commercial invoice, and freight invoice. Some countries also require documents such as an export declaration or packing list.

Can I apply for the ECTN with a draft Bill of Lading (BL) or a house BL and update it later?

Yes, AfricaCTN can often issue a draft CTN using a draft BL or house BL, though some countries require the master bill of lading before final validation.

Can you make changes to a validated ECTN Document?

Correction rules vary by country. Some authorities allow updates to a validated ECTN, while others do not.

Who is responsible for paying for the ECTN?

The shipper or freight forwarder typically handles the ECTN and should complete it before the validation deadline to avoid issues for the importer.

Are there exemptions for certain cargo types?

Exemptions are rare, so AfricaCTN recommends checking the destination-country requirements before assuming a shipment does not need an ECTN.
